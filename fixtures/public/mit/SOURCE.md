# Public MIT IFC samples

Two small IFC files copied from MIT-licensed repositories:

- `3walls.ifc` — [kazmi-y/Sample-IFC-Files](https://github.com/kazmi-y/Sample-IFC-Files), MIT; see `LICENSE-kazmi-y`.
- `SimpleWall.ifc` — [andrewisen/bim-whale-ifc-samples](https://github.com/andrewisen/bim-whale-ifc-samples), MIT; see `LICENSE-bim-whale`.

These are public structural samples, not customer evidence. They are **not counted** toward any SKU gate and are not shipped in the npm tarball.
